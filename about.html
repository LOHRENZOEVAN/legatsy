About Us

Out of Fule's frustration of juggling with prospecting, closing deals, managing existing clients, monitoring market trends and attending corporate meetings as a financial security advisor, her desire to master the marketing game grew by the day.

Fule has trained hundreds of agricultural entrepreneurs in the past. And she is very passionate about supporting small and medium business owners to start and scale their businesses through her marketing services.

Founded in March 2024, Legatsy Digital Marketing Agency (LDMA) is a brand name under 9512–0606 Québec inc., a company that was registered under the Québec Company Act.

Legatsy DMA's mission is to share the burden of small business owners by supporting them to find and manage clients effectively, so that they have time to serve their clients with love and attention.
